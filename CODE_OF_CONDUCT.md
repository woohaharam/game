# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Critique code, not the person who wrote it. Assume the
other person is acting in good faith until you have real evidence otherwise.

## What that means in practice

**Expected:** giving specific, actionable feedback; accepting feedback
gracefully; asking what someone meant before assuming the worst reading;
crediting other people's work.

**Not acceptable:** personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments;
sexualised language or imagery; publishing someone's private information;
sustained disruption of discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else that
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/woohaharam/game/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy, and may
block repeat offenders. Enforcement is proportionate: a first misstep usually
gets a private word, not a ban.
